# How do I export my orders to a CSV file?

You can download every order from a date range as one spreadsheet, ready for Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. It takes about a minute.

<!-- Reviewer note: every label below was copied from src/en.json. This comment never publishes. -->

## Before you start

You need access to the app's **Export orders** page.

## How to export your orders

1. Open **Export orders**.
2. Under **Date range**, pick the first and last day you want.
3. Under **File format**, keep **CSV**.
4. Click **Download CSV**.

## What each setting does

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| File format | CSV opens in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets. |
| Date range | Only orders placed inside this range are exported. |

## What this does NOT do

- It does not export more than **10,000 orders** in one file. Split a bigger range into two downloads.
- It does not email you the file. The download starts in your browser.

## Frequently asked

**Why is my file missing some orders?**

Only orders placed inside the date range are included, and one file stops at 10,000 orders. Check both.
